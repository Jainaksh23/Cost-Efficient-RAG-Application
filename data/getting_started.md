# Vaultly Getting Started Guide

**Welcome to Vaultly!** This guide walks you through your first 15 minutes with the platform.

---

## Step 1 — Log In

Open your browser and navigate to **dashboard.vaultly.io**. Enter the email address and password you used when creating your account. If you signed up via Google or Microsoft SSO, click the corresponding button on the login page instead.

If you forgot your password, click "Forgot password?" to receive a reset link by email. Password reset links expire after 1 hour.

---

## Step 2 — Create Your First Workspace

A workspace is an isolated container for your team's documents. On first login, you will be prompted to create a workspace. Click **"New Workspace"**, enter a name (e.g., your company name), and choose your preferred region (US East, US West, or EU West).

Each new workspace starts with 1 GB of default workspace storage on the free trial, which upgrades to your plan's full storage limit once a subscription is activated.

---

## Step 3 — Upload Your First Document

Click the **"Upload"** button in the top toolbar. Drag and drop files onto the upload zone, or click to browse your file system. You can upload multiple files at once. Supported formats include PDF, DOCX, XLSX, PNG, JPG, and MP4.

After upload, Vaultly automatically indexes the document for full-text search. Indexing typically completes within 30 seconds for documents under 10 MB.

---

## Step 4 — Invite Team Members

To add colleagues to your workspace, navigate to **Settings > Team > Invite**. Enter one or more email addresses (comma-separated) and select a role:

- **Viewer:** Can view and download documents.
- **Editor:** Can upload, edit, and delete documents.
- **Admin:** Full access including billing and workspace settings.

Invited users receive an email with a link to join. The link expires after 48 hours.

---

## Step 5 — Organize with Folders

Create folders to keep your documents organized. Click **"New Folder"** in the left sidebar. Folders can be nested up to 5 levels deep. Documents can belong to only one folder at a time but can be tagged with multiple labels for cross-cutting organization.

---

## Roles and Permissions

Only the Admin role can manage billing, change the subscription plan, and invite or remove team members. Editors and Viewers cannot access the billing portal.

---
