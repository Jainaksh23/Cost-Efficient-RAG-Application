About Vaultly

Our Mission

Vaultly was founded in 2019 with a single purpose: to make secure document collaboration accessible to every team, regardless of size or technical expertise. We believe that organizations should never have to choose between security and usability.

Company Facts

  • Founded: 2019
  • Headquarters: Austin, Texas
  • CEO: Sarah Mitchell
  • Team Size: 85 employees
  • Funding: Series B, $42 million raised to date
  • Customers: Over 3,200 organizations in 47 countries

Leadership

Vaultly is led by CEO Sarah Mitchell, a former engineering director at Dropbox, and CTO Marcus Webb, who previously built document processing infrastructure at Adobe Systems. The leadership team brings over 40 years of combined experience in cloud storage, security, and enterprise software.

Our Values

  • Security First: Every feature is designed with security as the primary constraint.
  • Radical Transparency: We publish our security audits and uptime history publicly.
  • Customer Obsession: Our Net Promoter Score (NPS) of 71 reflects genuine care for users.
  • Sustainable Growth: We grow responsibly, without compromising product quality.

Certifications and Compliance

Vaultly is SOC 2 Type II certified, GDPR compliant, and HIPAA eligible (for Enterprise customers with a signed Business Associate Agreement). We undergo annual third-party security audits and publish a transparency report every six months.
