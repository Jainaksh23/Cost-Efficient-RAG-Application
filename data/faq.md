# Vaultly Frequently Asked Questions

**Last Updated:** February 20, 2025

---

## General

**Q: What file formats does Vaultly support?**
A: Vaultly supports the following file formats for upload and storage: PDF, DOCX, XLSX, PNG, JPG, and MP4. Support for additional formats is planned for future releases.

**Q: Is there a limit on how many documents I can store?**
A: There is no limit on the number of individual documents. Storage is limited only by the total GB included in your plan (10 GB for Starter, 100 GB for Professional, 1 TB for Enterprise).

**Q: Can I share documents with people outside my workspace?**
A: Yes. You can generate a shareable link for any document. Links can be set to view-only or allow downloads. You can also set an expiration date on shared links.

---

## Security

**Q: Does Vaultly support two-factor authentication?**
A: Yes. Two-factor authentication (2FA) is supported via any TOTP-compatible authenticator app such as Google Authenticator or Authy. To enable 2FA, go to Settings > Security > Enable 2FA.

**Q: How is my data encrypted?**
A: All data is encrypted at rest using AES-256 and in transit using TLS 1.3. Encryption keys are managed by Vaultly using AWS Key Management Service (KMS). Enterprise customers may bring their own encryption keys (BYOK).

**Q: Is Vaultly SOC 2 certified?**
A: Yes. Vaultly holds a SOC 2 Type II certification. The certification report is available to Enterprise customers upon request under NDA.

---

## Features

**Q: Can I access Vaultly on my mobile device?**
A: Yes. The Vaultly mobile app is available for iOS and Android. Download it from the App Store or Google Play Store. The mobile app supports document viewing, uploading photos, and commenting.

**Q: What export formats are available?**
A: You can export your workspace data as PDF (individual documents), CSV (metadata and search results), or ZIP (bulk document download). Exports are available from Settings > Export Data.

**Q: Does Vaultly support version control?**
A: Yes. Every time a document is updated, a new version is created automatically. You can view, compare, and restore any previous version from the document's Version History panel. Starter plans retain the last 30 days of version history; Professional and Enterprise retain unlimited version history.

---

## Billing

**Q: Can I change my plan at any time?**
A: Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated. Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing cycle.

**Q: What payment methods are accepted?**
A: Vaultly accepts Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and bank transfers (ACH) for Enterprise annual plans. All payments are processed securely through Stripe.
